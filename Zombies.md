These are instructions on how to play the '''Zombies TC ''' under Chocolate Doom.

# Obtain a copy of the TC [http://www.doomworld.com/idgames/?id=8122 here].
# Download '''zombies.zip''' from one of the mirror sites.
# Extract the zip archive. 
# Run with the following [[command-line arguments]]:

 chocolate-doom -deh zombie.deh -merge doom2.wad zombie.wad

''Additional Note(s)'': Since this TC was originally made for Doom 2, it is compatible with the [[Freedoom|Freedoom IWAD]], you won't even have to purchase anything just to play and try this TC out. Especially for all the Zombies, Romero, any other "Dead" (or should I say Un-Dead) films fans in particular. I know you all out there might want to give this a try and not all of you own the original Doom games.
