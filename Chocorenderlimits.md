'''Chocorenderlimits''' is a [[:Category:Forks|fork]] of Chocolate Doom that adds an on-screen display that shows information about Vanilla Doom rendering limits.  This is intended to assist level authors in creating levels for Vanilla Doom, by identifying areas of the levels that will crash the game.
