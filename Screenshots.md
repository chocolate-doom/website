== Multiplatform ==

[[Image:Chocolate-doom.png]] [[Image:chocowindows.png]]

Chocolate Doom supports multiple Operating Systems, including [[Wikipedia:Linux|Linux]] and [[Wikipedia:Microsoft Windows|Microsoft Windows]].
