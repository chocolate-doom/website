Forks of Chocolate Doom are ports derived from the Chocolate Doom codebase. Unlike [:Category:Branches](/Category:Branches.md), forks typically have different design goals to Chocolate Doom, and will not be merged back in the future.

Chocolate Doom is specifically designed and intended to be easily forkable.
