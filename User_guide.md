## Online assistance {#online_assistance}

You can get help online from several sources:

Discord [Chocolate Doomslayers](https://discord.gg/fsVZfEE)
: Active Discord guild with 200 and growing members.

Doomworld forums [discussion thread](https://www.doomworld.com/vb/source-ports/86366-chocolate-doom/)
: The Doomworld forums contain many experts on the subject who may be able to answer your questions.

Email [the Chocolate Doom development mailing list](mailto:chocolate-doom-dev-list@chocolate-doom.org)
: Contact the authors of the source port directly.

## Bugs

If you discover a bug, please [submit a bug report](https://github.com/chocolate-doom/chocolate-doom/issues). However, some bugs are actually intended behavior. See the NOT-BUGS file that was included in your download (viewable {% include Srclink.md filename="NOT-BUGS.md" text="here" %}).
