Chocolate Doom version 2 is now in beta. You can help out by testing the beta and [reporting bugs](https://sourceforge.net/p/chocolate-doom/bugs/) that you encounter.

Version 2 has been in development for several years now. The biggest change is the addition of support for Heretic, Hexen and Strife in addition to Doom. See [the NEWS file](https://sourceforge.net/p/chocolate-doom/code/HEAD/tree/tags/chocolate-doom-2.0.0-beta3/NEWS) for more information.

All of the downloads below are GPG-signed. See the [downloads directory](http://www.chocolate-doom.org/downloads/2.0.0-beta3/) for the .asc format detached signatures.

## Windows binaries {#windows_binaries}

Downloads can be found here:

- [Chocolate Doom v2.0.0-beta3](http://www.chocolate-doom.org/downloads/2.0.0-beta3/chocolate-doom-2.0.0-beta3-win32.zip)
- [Chocolate Heretic v2.0.0-beta3](http://www.chocolate-doom.org/downloads/2.0.0-beta3/chocolate-heretic-2.0.0-beta3-win32.zip)
- [Chocolate Hexen v2.0.0-beta3](http://www.chocolate-doom.org/downloads/2.0.0-beta3/chocolate-hexen-2.0.0-beta3-win32.zip)
- [Chocolate Strife v2.0.0-beta3](http://www.chocolate-doom.org/downloads/2.0.0-beta3/chocolate-strife-2.0.0-beta3-win32.zip)

## Mac OS X binaries {#mac_os_x_binaries}
