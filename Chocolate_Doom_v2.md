Chocolate Doom version 2 is now in beta. You can help out by testing the beta and [https://sourceforge.net/p/chocolate-doom/bugs/ reporting bugs] that you encounter.

Version 2 has been in development for several years now. The biggest change is the addition of support for Heretic, Hexen and Strife in addition to Doom. See [https://sourceforge.net/p/chocolate-doom/code/HEAD/tree/tags/chocolate-doom-2.0.0-beta2/NEWS the NEWS file] for more information.

All of the downloads below are GPG-signed. See the [https://soulsphere.org/projects/chocolate-doom/2.0.0-beta2/ downloads directory] for the .asc format detached signatures.
