1998 saw the public release of the [Doom source code](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Doom_source_code) by its original authors, id Software. Since then, a large number of [Doom \"source ports\"](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Source_port) have been created by programmers, who have extended the code, adding new features and removing some of the limitations of DOS Doom (now commonly referred to as \"Vanilla Doom\" by fans). These efforts should be commended, and many of the modern Doom source ports are truly impressive pieces of work.

Chocolate Doom takes a different approach to other source ports. Its aim is to accurately reproduce the experience of playing Vanilla Doom. It is a conservative, historically accurate Doom source port, which is [compatible](/Compatibility.md) with the thousands of mods and levels that were made before the Doom source code was released. Rather than flashy new graphics, Chocolate Doom\'s main [features](/Category:Features.md) are its accurate reproduction of the game as it was played in the 1990s. The project is developed around a carefully-considered [philosophy](/Philosophy.md) that intentionally restricts which features may be added (and rejects any that affect gameplay).

Originally just a port of the Doom source code, the project has grown to include ports of [Heretic](/Chocolate_Heretic.md), [Hexen](/Chocolate_Hexen.md) and [Strife](/Chocolate_Strife.md), other commercial games that were based on the Doom engine.

The source code to Chocolate Doom is [Free Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Software) under the [GNU General Public License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_GPL), and is [portable](/Portability.md) to a variety of different operating systems. It therefore preserves the gameplay of the original game for modern computers, many of which are now unable to run the original.

## Authors

Chocolate Doom is written by [Simon Howard](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Simon_Howard_(Fraggle)), with extensive help from many other people who have provided patches, bug reports and suggestions.

Chocolate Strife was developed by [James Haley](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/James_Haley_(Quasar)) and [Samuel Villarreal](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Samuel_Villarreal_(Kaiser)) as part of a painstaking reverse engineering project ([read more about this](/Chocolate_Strife.md)).

The Chocolate Doom icon (as shown at the top left of this page) is one of [a series of chocolate-themed icons](/Category:Clovehoof_icons.md) kindly [created for the project by Github user \"clovehoof\"](https://github.com/chocolate-doom/chocolate-doom/issues/1020#issuecomment-557849165). Chocolate Doom\'s previous icon was based on a photo of an icecream by Chris Metcalf.
