'''Strawberry Doom''' is a fork of the Chocolate Doom codebase that extends some of the limits of Chocolate Doom to match some of those of Doom+ [http://prboom-plus.sourceforge.net/], as well as adding features such as playable bots. 

Development of Strawberry Doom appears to have halted.
