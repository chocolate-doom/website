Russian Doom is aimed to full and proper Russian translation of Doom, Heretic and Hexen games, as well as its setup executable and online help. Additionally it has numerous vanilla bug fixes, removed limits and offers some optional aesthetic game enhancements. 

The project is based on [[Chocolate Doom]] and [[Crispy Doom]]

== Supported games ==

The following games are supported. It is recommended to only play the original game series.

* DOOM Shareware
* DOOM 1
* The Ultimate DOOM
* DOOM II: Hell on Earth
* DOOM II: No Rest for the Living
* Final DOOM: TNT Evilution
* Final DOOM: The Plutonia Experiment
* Heretic Shareware (Beta)
* Heretic Shareware
* Heretic
* Heretic: Shadows of the Serpent Riders
* Hexen Shareware
* Hexen
* Hexen: Deathkings of the Dark Citadel

No need to use “<tt>-file</tt>” for loading translated resources, the port will automatically load them depending on the game you are playing.

== Game enhancements ==

All enhancements are optional and may be disabled to get the completely original feeling of the DOS version. It can be done in the ex-compatibility section of the Setup executable and all of them are available only for Doom. 

'''Graphics:'''

* Colored blood and squished corpses
* Correct formula of "Ouch face"
* invulnerability affects sky
* Diabolical-red flash of monsters resurrection in Nightmare skill level
* Super Shotgun has a chance to blast enemies
* Very low-key transparency
* No yellow flash on picking up items (disabled by default)

'''Sounds:'''

* Sound of crushed corpses
* Single sound of fast closing doors
* Playing sound on exiting the game

'''Gameplay:'''

* Notification of discovered secrets
* Total level times on intermission screen
* Pain Elemental without Lost Soul's attack limit
