As inspired by [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaghetti_Western Spaghetti Westerns], notably the three films of the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dollars_Trilogy Dollars Trilogy] (starring Clint Eastwood), '''Fistful of Doom''' is a wild west themed Doom TC that has new sounds and graphics.

For [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Doomworld Doomworld]'s ''[https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Top_100_WADs_of_All_Time Top 100 WADs of All Time]'' list, [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Andrew_Stine_%28Linguica%29 Andrew Stine (Linguica)] [https://www.doomworld.com/10years/bestwads/1995.php wrote that] "[Fistful of Doom is a] great examples of the creative spirit found in early WAD makers where completely off the wall ideas could actually be done quite well within the confines of the Doom engine".

==To play under Chocolate Doom:==

# Download {{idgames|file=levels/doom2/d-f/fistful3.zip|title=Fistful of Doom}}.
# Extract the .zip containing the game files.
# Run with the following command line parameters:

 chocolate-doom -merge fistful.wad -file fistfix.wad
