As inspired by [Spaghetti Westerns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaghetti_Western), notably the three films of the [Dollars Trilogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dollars_Trilogy) (starring Clint Eastwood), **Fistful of Doom** is a wild west themed Doom TC that has new sounds and graphics.

For [Doomworld](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Doomworld)\'s *[Top 100 WADs of All Time](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Top_100_WADs_of_All_Time)* list, [Andrew Stine (Linguica)](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Andrew_Stine_%28Linguica%29) [wrote that](https://www.doomworld.com/10years/bestwads/1995.php) \"\[Fistful of Doom is a\] great examples of the creative spirit found in early WAD makers where completely off the wall ideas could actually be done quite well within the confines of the Doom engine\".

## To play under Chocolate Doom: {#to_play_under_chocolate_doom}

1.  Download {% include Idgames.md file="levels/doom2/d-f/fistful3.zip" title="Fistful of Doom" %}.
2.  Extract the .zip containing the game files.
3.  Run with the following command line parameters:

<!-- -->

    chocolate-doom -merge fistful.wad -file fistfix.wad
