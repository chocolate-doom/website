## Doom Minor Sprite Fixing Project {#doom_minor_sprite_fixing_project}

The Sprite Fixing Project provides minor adjustments to fix issues with some of Doom\'s sprites, such as:

- Fixes for single-pixel graphical errors
- Adjustment of sprite offsets to improve animation of certain monsters
- Addition of extra rotation angles for zombie monsters which were omitted from the official IWADs.

## Perkristian hi-res Doom sound pack {#perkristian_hi_res_doom_sound_pack}

The hi-res sound pack contains remastered versions of the Doom sound effects which have been remade at higher sampling rates (Doom\'s original sound effects were sampled at 11025Hz, which is considered fairly low-quality nowadays).

### External links {#external_links_1}

- [Website with more information](http://www.perkristian.net/game_doom-sfx.shtml) including download.
- [First](https://www.doomworld.com/vb/wads-mods/45117-high-quality-original-doom-sfx-updated-01-08-10/) and [second](https://www.doomworld.com/vb/wads-mods/58879-the-hi-res-doom-sound-pack-is-updated/) Doomworld threads about the mod.

## Roland SC-55 music packs {#roland_sc_55_music_packs}

These downloadable music packs contain high quality recordings of Doom\'s music played through a [Roland SC-55](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_SC-55), the MIDI synthesizer used by [Bobby Prince](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Robert_Prince) when composing Doom\'s music. Usable with Chocolate Doom\'s [digital music packs](/Digital_music_packs.md) feature.

### External links {#external_links_2}

- [Website](http://sc55.duke4.net/) with downloads for the music packs.

## EAWPATS sound font {#eawpats_sound_font}

There are a wide variety of options available for music playback with Chocolate Doom. One good option is to use [Timidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TiMidity++), and the EAWPATS patch set makes for a good time-tested starter set.

## Vanilla Doom smooth weapons patch {#vanilla_doom_smooth_weapons_patch}

The vanilla Doom smooth weapons patch allows users to play [Smooth Doom](https://forum.zdoom.org/viewtopic.php?t=45550) (a mod not made for vanilla ports) on vanilla ports. This patch should work fine with most PWADs, along with most of the IWADs supported by Chocolate Doom, however, the patch might not work as intended (or at all) while using a [DeHackEd file (.deh)](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/DeHackEd).

The patch should not cause desyncing while [recording or playing back a demo](https://www.chocolate-doom.org/wiki/index.php/Command_line_arguments#Demo_options).
