''Doom 64 for Doom II'' is a 2018 port for the 1990s cult Nintendo 64 classic, Doom 64, recreated and reimagined with vanilla Doom in mind. Not all of the maps are a perfect recreation of the original game; the goal of the mod was to recreate the major aspects of the game while allowing for creative liberties where needed or wanted.

On its own, the mod replaces very few assets, making playing it on its own a very Doom II feeling game, rather than Doom 64. You can make the over-all feeling of the mod more Doom 64-like by running it aside Doomkid's ''Doom64 Sprites & Sounds for Vanilla Doom2/Final Doom'', a vanilla-compatible compilation of all the Doom 64 sprites and sound effects, by doing this, you will have effectively made a bootleg "port" of the game.

=== Instructions ===
# Download {{idgames|file=levels/doom2/megawads/d64d2.zip|title=Doom 64 for Doom II}} and extract the .zip file.
# Next, download [http://doomshack.org/wads/d64-gfx.zip Doom64 Sprites & Sounds] ([http://web.archive.org/web/20200701204933/https://doomshack.org/wads/d64-gfx.zip mirror]) and extract the .zip file.
# Run with these options:

 chocolate-doom -iwad doom2.wad -merge d64d2.deh D64D2_v1.4.wad d64-gfx.wad -deh
