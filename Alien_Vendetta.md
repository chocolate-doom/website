{{Doomwiki|Alien Vendetta}}
2001 saw the release of '''Alien Vendetta''' ('''av.wad'''), a 32-level single player megawad for Vanilla Doom, and one of the few PWADs that are allowed to be used in [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Compet-n Compet-n speedruns].

In 2003, as part of [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Doomworld Doomworld]'s ''[https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Top_100_WADs_of_All_Time Top 100 WADs of All Time]'' list by [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Andrew_Stine_%28Linguica%29 Andrew Stine (Linguica)] and [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Mike_Watson_%28Cyb%29 Mike Watson (Cyb)], Watson [https://www.doomworld.com/10years/bestwads/2002.php wrote that] "AV is simply an amazing effort and indeed deserving of a place here [on this list]. In fact, I (and many others) would go as far to put AV in the top ten wads of all time. Even if the gameplay isn't your thing, you will simply not find a better collection of 32 maps, and you probably won't ever again".
