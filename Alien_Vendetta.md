2001 saw the release of **Alien Vendetta** (**av.wad**), a 32-level single player megawad for Vanilla Doom, and one of the few PWADs that are allowed to be used in [Compet-n speedruns](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Compet-n).

In 2003, as part of [Doomworld](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Doomworld)\'s *[Top 100 WADs of All Time](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Top_100_WADs_of_All_Time)* list by [Andrew Stine (Linguica)](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Andrew_Stine_%28Linguica%29) and [Mike Watson (Cyb)](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Mike_Watson_%28Cyb%29), Watson [wrote that](https://www.doomworld.com/10years/bestwads/2002.php) \"AV is simply an amazing effort and indeed deserving of a place here \[on this list\]. In fact, I (and many others) would go as far to put AV in the top ten wads of all time. Even if the gameplay isn\'t your thing, you will simply not find a better collection of 32 maps, and you probably won\'t ever again\".
