As named after [http://sodomized.info/ Sodom], a German thrash metal band, ''So Doom'' is a friendly fork of [[Crispy_Doom | Crispy Doom]] (that in itself is a port of Chocolate Doom). According to its [https://github.com/Zodomaniac/So-Doom/blob/master/README.md README file], the port "aims to provide a faithful Doom gaming experience while also featuring some user-requested improvements and enhancements. It is forked off of Crispy Doom to take advantage of its free and open-source code base, portability, accuracy and compatibility with Vanilla Doom".

The port removes the [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Static_limits static limits] issue that effects some Doom ports including the [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Visplane_overflow visplane overflow], a fatal error that occurs in [https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Doom_engine#Vanilla_Doom vanilla Doom] when there are more than 128 unique floor and ceiling surfaces (visplanes) on the screen at the same time, the port also includes a singleplayer-only optional removal of INTERCEPTS limit

Along with this, So Doom adds other new things such as having widescreen, new cheat codes, and an in-game control of pixel aspect ratio correction, meaning that the user does not need to manually edit the <tt>so-doom.cfg</tt> file.
