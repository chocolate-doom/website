As named after [Sodom](http://sodomized.info/), a German thrash metal band, *So Doom* is a friendly fork of [ Crispy Doom](/Crispy_Doom.md) (that in itself is a port of Chocolate Doom). According to its [README file](https://github.com/Zodomaniac/So-Doom/blob/master/README.md), the port \"aims to provide a faithful Doom gaming experience while also featuring some user-requested improvements and enhancements. It is forked off of Crispy Doom to take advantage of its free and open-source code base, portability, accuracy and compatibility with Vanilla Doom\".

The port removes the [static limits](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Static_limits) issue that effects some Doom ports including the [visplane overflow](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Visplane_overflow), a fatal error that occurs in [vanilla Doom](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Doom_engine#Vanilla_Doom) when there are more than 128 unique floor and ceiling surfaces (visplanes) on the screen at the same time, the port also includes a singleplayer-only optional removal of INTERCEPTS limit

Along with this, So Doom adds other new things such as having widescreen, new cheat codes, and an in-game control of pixel aspect ratio correction, meaning that the user does not need to manually edit the `so-doom.cfg` file.
