== Overview ==

Doomwire is a multiplayer lobby program for Chocolate-Doom created by Russell Rice.
It was created as an alternative to DoomServ a few years after the program was "shut down"

Originally designed for ZDoom, it has now been changed in favour of Chocolate-Doom for
the authors craving of oldschool deathmatch and cooperative.
